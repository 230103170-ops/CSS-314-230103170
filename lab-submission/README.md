Experiments ran on an Apple M4 with 10 physical cores and 128-byte cache lines. Stride latency was approximately 49–50 ns per element for strides 1–64 and 121.73 ns at stride 128; Python overhead limits cache-level interpretation. Loop interchange produced no speedup: i-j-k took 56.39 s and i-k-j took 69.52 s, with identical numerical outputs. At 8 processes, shared counters took 0.5076 s without padding, 0.4007 s with 128-byte padding, and 0.1867 s with privatization; all counts were correct. Strong scaling peaked at 1.257x with 2 processes. Weak-scaling throughput reached 2.323x at 10 processes. Process startup and synchronization are included in timings, and the fitted Amdahl model does not adequately describe the measurements. The stencil has low estimated arithmetic intensity, but a definitive Roofline classification requires hardware peak values and memory-traffic measurements.
